① The Story

A Colorado family of builders

We're a family from Colorado — a father who spent decades finding worth in buildings other people had given up on, and sons who grew up watching him do it. Real estate, software, music, ministry: different trades, same table.

The oldest son spent one summer working with Dad at Cadence Capital — and learned something true about himself: he couldn't stand being in an office all day, looking at buildings as commercial real estate deals. It wasn't for him. He went the artistic route. His two younger brothers stayed close to their roots and became successful real estate gurus in their own right.

The brothers built careers, then wives and kids. The oldest stayed a bachelor — a bard traveling the world with a ukulele, following the builders of Ethereum from city to city. And somewhere along that road he was given an unexpected gift: he was taught how to give creative life to AI agents.

In 2026 the family reunited around one idea. The brothers' success had a price — hours taken from their wives and sons. And their brother's agents could give those hours back: AI agents can be honest employees. Not toys, not chatbots — employees. Given a desk, a clear job, and a boss who checks their work, an agent can prospect, research, write, and report for a working business the way a good junior hire would — except it never sleeps and it shows its sources. Ham Agents exists so the brothers can be fathers and husbands first — and so every property manager and seller can run their business seamlessly with an AI agent family of their own.

② The Affordability Gospel

④ The Creed

What the family believes

  • Honesty. Every claim our agents make is cited to a source a human can click. No citation, no claim. We even publish what our agents are still bad at, in public, at terribleagents.com.
  • Humans approve everything. Agents propose; people decide. No outreach, no spending, no promises leave the desk without a human hand on the send button.
  • Family forever. Ham Agents is family-built and family-owned. We answer to the people at our own dinner table, and we build like our name is on the door — because it is.
